Discover the Beauty of Mt. Napulak

Welcome to Mt. Napulak, one of the beautiful mountain destinations in Igbaras, Iloilo, Philippines. It is known for its unique rocky summit, green mountain ranges, and amazing views of the surrounding landscape.Many hikers visit this mountain to enjoy nature and experience an exciting outdoor adventure.

Mt. Napulak is a wonderful place for people who love hiking, taking pictures, and exploring nature. Visitors can enjoy the fresh air, peaceful surroundings, and amazing mountain scenery. Everyone is encouraged to keep the mountain clean and protect its plants and animals.

Our website shares the beauty of Mt. Napulak through photographs and information. We hope to inspire visitors to explore nature while keeping the mountain clean and protecting its natural surroundings.
