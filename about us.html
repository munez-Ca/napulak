About Us

Welcome to the official digital gallery of Mt. Napulak, a beautiful mountain located in Igbaras, Iloilo, Philippines. Known for its rocky peak that looks like a mortar, Mt. Napulak is a popular destination for hikers and nature lovers. Our mission is to showcase the mountain's natural beauty, amazing landscapes, and unique rock formations to local and international visitors.

Through photography and stories, we aim to promote responsible tourism and encourage people to appreciate and protect nature. Whether you are an experienced hiker or someone who loves beautiful mountain views, we invite you to discover the beauty and adventure of Mt. Napulak.
